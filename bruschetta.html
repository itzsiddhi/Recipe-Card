Classic Bruschetta

20 mins 6 servings Easy

Fresh and vibrant Italian appetizer with ripe tomatoes, basil, and garlic on crispy toasted bread. This classic bruschetta is perfect for entertaining and showcases the beauty of simple, quality ingredients.

Ready to start cooking!
00:00

Ingredients

  • 1 loaf Italian bread, sliced 1/2 inch thick
  • 4 large ripe tomatoes, diced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced (plus 2 whole cloves)
  • 1/4 cup fresh basil, chopped
  • 3 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • 2 tbsp balsamic vinegar
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper
  • 1/4 cup parmesan cheese, grated (optional)

Instructions

  1. 1
    Prepare tomato mixture: In a bowl, combine diced tomatoes, minced garlic, chopped basil, olive oil, balsamic vinegar, salt, and pepper. Mix well and let sit for 10 minutes.
  2. 2
    Toast bread: Preheat oven to 450°F (230°C). Place bread slices on a baking sheet and toast for 5-6 minutes until golden and crispy.
  3. 3
    Rub with garlic: While bread is still warm, rub each slice with the whole garlic cloves to infuse flavor.
  4. 4
    Drain tomatoes: Use a slotted spoon to drain excess liquid from the tomato mixture for better texture.
  5. 5
    Top bread: Spoon the tomato mixture generously onto each slice of toasted bread.
  6. 6
    Finish and serve: Sprinkle with parmesan cheese if desired and serve immediately while the bread is still crispy.

Pro Tips

  • Use the ripest tomatoes you can find for the best flavor
  • Let the tomato mixture sit to develop flavors before serving
  • Serve immediately to maintain bread crispness
  • Try different bread types like ciabatta or sourdough
  • Add a drizzle of good olive oil just before serving
