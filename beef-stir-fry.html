Asian Beef Stir Fry

20 mins 4 servings Easy

Quick and flavorful beef stir fry with vegetables and savory sauce. Perfect for a weeknight dinner that's both nutritious and delicious.

Ready to start cooking!
00:00

Ingredients

  • 1 lb beef sirloin, sliced thin
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 large bell pepper, sliced
  • 1 large onion, sliced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp oyster sauce
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 2 cups steamed rice

Instructions

  1. 1
    Heat the wok: Heat oil in a large wok or skillet over high heat until smoking.
  2. 2
    Cook the beef: Add beef and cook for 2-3 minutes until browned on all sides.
  3. 3
    Add vegetables: Add bell pepper and onion, stir-fry for 2 minutes until crisp-tender.
  4. 4
    Add aromatics: Add garlic and cook for 30 seconds until fragrant.
  5. 5
    Make sauce: Mix soy sauce, oyster sauce, and sesame oil in a small bowl.
  6. 6
    Finish and serve: Pour sauce over the stir-fry and toss everything together. Serve immediately over steamed rice.

Pro Tips

  • Cut beef against the grain for maximum tenderness
  • Have all ingredients prepped before you start cooking
  • High heat is key for proper stir-frying
  • Don't overcrowd the pan or vegetables will steam
