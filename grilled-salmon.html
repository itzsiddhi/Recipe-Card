Grilled Salmon

25 mins 4 servings Medium

Perfectly grilled salmon with a crispy exterior and tender, flaky interior. This healthy and delicious dish is seasoned with herbs and lemon for a fresh, restaurant-quality meal at home.

Ready to start cooking!
00:00

Ingredients

  • 4 fillets salmon (6 oz each), skin-on
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tbsp fresh lemon juice
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1 lemon, sliced for garnish
  • 2 tbsp fresh dill, chopped

Instructions

  1. 1
    Prepare marinade: In a bowl, whisk together olive oil, lemon juice, minced garlic, thyme, paprika, salt, and pepper.
  2. 2
    Marinate salmon: Place salmon fillets in a dish and pour marinade over them. Let marinate for 15 minutes at room temperature.
  3. 3
    Preheat grill: Heat grill to medium-high heat (about 400°F). Clean and oil the grill grates to prevent sticking.
  4. 4
    Grill skin-side down: Place salmon skin-side down on the grill. Cook for 4-5 minutes without moving to get nice grill marks.
  5. 5
    Flip and finish: Carefully flip salmon using a wide spatula. Grill for 3-4 minutes more until fish flakes easily with a fork.
  6. 6
    Garnish and serve: Remove from grill and garnish with fresh dill and lemon slices. Serve immediately while hot.

Pro Tips

  • Don't flip the salmon too early - let it develop a crust first
  • Use a fish spatula for easier flipping without breaking
  • Check doneness by gently pressing - it should flake easily
  • Let the fish rest for 2-3 minutes before serving
  • Serve with grilled vegetables or rice for a complete meal
