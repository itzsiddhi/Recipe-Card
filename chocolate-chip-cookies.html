Chocolate Chip Cookies

25 mins 24 cookies Easy

Classic homemade chocolate chip cookies that are crispy on the edges and soft in the center. These perfectly balanced cookies are loaded with chocolate chips and have that irresistible homemade taste.

Ready to start cooking!
00:00

Ingredients

  • 2¼ cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 cup butter, softened
  • ¾ cup granulated sugar
  • ¾ cup brown sugar, packed
  • 2 large eggs
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 2 cups chocolate chips

Instructions

  1. 1
    Preheat oven: Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C). Line baking sheets with parchment paper or lightly grease them.
  2. 2
    Mix dry ingredients: In a medium bowl, whisk together flour, baking soda, and salt. Set aside.
  3. 3
    Cream butter and sugars: In a large bowl, cream together softened butter, granulated sugar, and brown sugar until light and fluffy, about 3 minutes.
  4. 4
    Add eggs and vanilla: Beat in eggs one at a time, then add vanilla extract. Mix until well combined.
  5. 5
    Combine ingredients: Gradually blend in the flour mixture, then stir in the chocolate chips until evenly distributed.
  6. 6
    Shape and bake: Drop rounded tablespoons of dough 2 inches apart on prepared baking sheets. Bake for 9-11 minutes until golden brown.
  7. 7
    Cool and serve: Cool on baking sheets for 2 minutes, then transfer to wire racks to cool completely.

Pro Tips

  • Use room temperature ingredients for better mixing
  • Don't overbake - cookies will continue cooking on the hot pan
  • Chill dough for 30 minutes for thicker cookies
  • Use parchment paper to prevent sticking
  • Store in airtight container for up to a week
