Tropical Smoothie Bowl

15 mins 2 servings Easy

A refreshing and nutritious smoothie bowl packed with tropical fruits and topped with crunchy granola, fresh fruit, and coconut. Perfect for a healthy breakfast or post-workout treat.

Ready to start cooking!
00:00

Ingredients

For the Smoothie Base:

  • 1 cup frozen mango chunks
  • 1/2 cup frozen pineapple chunks
  • 1 frozen banana
  • 1/2 cup coconut milk
  • 1 tbsp honey (optional)

For Toppings:

  • 1/4 cup granola
  • 2 tbsp coconut flakes
  • 1/2 kiwi, sliced
  • 1/4 cup fresh berries
  • 1 tbsp chia seeds
  • 2 tbsp sliced almonds

Instructions

  1. 1
    Prepare toppings: Slice the kiwi, wash the berries, and arrange all toppings in separate bowls for easy assembly.
  2. 2
    Blend smoothie base: In a high-speed blender, combine frozen mango, pineapple, banana, coconut milk, and honey (if using).
  3. 3
    Blend until thick: Blend on high speed until smooth and thick like soft-serve ice cream. Add more coconut milk if needed, but keep it thick.
  4. 4
    Pour into bowls: Divide the smoothie mixture between 2 serving bowls, creating a thick, creamy base.
  5. 5
    Add toppings: Arrange toppings in colorful sections on top: granola, coconut flakes, sliced kiwi, berries, chia seeds, and almonds.
  6. 6
    Serve immediately: Serve right away while the smoothie base is cold and thick. Enjoy with a spoon!

Pro Tips

  • Use frozen fruit for the best thick consistency
  • Don't add too much liquid - keep the base thick
  • Arrange toppings in sections for an Instagram-worthy presentation
  • Prep all toppings before blending for quick assembly
  • Try different fruit combinations for variety
