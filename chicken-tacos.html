Chicken Tacos

30 mins 4 servings Easy

Flavorful and juicy chicken tacos with perfectly seasoned meat, fresh toppings, and warm tortillas. These authentic-style tacos are perfect for a quick weeknight dinner or casual entertaining.

Ready to start cooking!
00:00

Ingredients

For the Chicken:

  • 1 lb chicken breast, sliced thin
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp chili powder
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper

For Assembly:

  • 8 small corn or flour tortillas
  • 1 cup lettuce, shredded
  • 1 tomato, diced
  • 1/2 cup cheese, shredded
  • 1/4 cup sour cream
  • 1 lime, cut into wedges
  • 1/4 cup cilantro, chopped

Instructions

  1. 1
    Season chicken: In a bowl, combine chili powder, cumin, paprika, garlic powder, salt, and pepper. Toss chicken with olive oil and spice mixture.
  2. 2
    Cook chicken: Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat. Cook seasoned chicken for 6-8 minutes, stirring occasionally, until fully cooked and golden.
  3. 3
    Warm tortillas: Heat tortillas in a dry skillet for 30 seconds on each side, or wrap in damp paper towels and microwave for 30 seconds.
  4. 4
    Prepare toppings: Set up all toppings in small bowls: shredded lettuce, diced tomato, cheese, sour cream, and chopped cilantro.
  5. 5
    Assemble tacos: Place chicken in warm tortillas. Top with lettuce, tomato, cheese, and a dollop of sour cream.
  6. 6
    Finish and serve: Garnish with fresh cilantro and serve immediately with lime wedges on the side.

Pro Tips

  • Don't overcook the chicken - it should be juicy and tender
  • Let the chicken rest for 2-3 minutes before slicing
  • Warm tortillas for better flexibility and flavor
  • Set up a taco bar so everyone can customize their tacos
  • Add hot sauce or salsa for extra flavor
