New York Cheesecake

60 mins 10 servings Hard

Rich, creamy, and perfectly dense - this classic New York-style cheesecake is the ultimate dessert. With a graham cracker crust and silky smooth filling, it's worth every minute of preparation.

Ready to start cooking!
00:00

Ingredients

For the Crust:

  • 1½ cups graham cracker crumbs
  • ¼ cup sugar
  • ⅓ cup melted butter

For the Filling:

  • 32 oz cream cheese, room temperature
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 4 large eggs, room temperature
  • ⅓ cup sour cream
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 3 tbsp all-purpose flour

Instructions

  1. 1
    Prepare oven and pan: Preheat oven to 325°F (165°C). Grease a 9-inch springform pan and wrap the outside with aluminum foil.
  2. 2
    Make crust: Mix graham cracker crumbs, sugar, and melted butter. Press into bottom of prepared pan and bake for 10 minutes.
  3. 3
    Beat cream cheese: In a large bowl, beat cream cheese until smooth and fluffy, about 3 minutes. Scrape bowl frequently.
  4. 4
    Add sugar and eggs: Beat in sugar until smooth. Add eggs one at a time, beating just until combined after each addition.
  5. 5
    Mix remaining ingredients: Beat in sour cream, vanilla, and flour until just combined. Don't overmix.
  6. 6
    Pour and bake: Pour filling over crust. Place pan in a water bath and bake for 55-60 minutes until center is almost set.
  7. 7
    Cool completely: Turn off oven and crack door. Let cool for 1 hour, then refrigerate for at least 4 hours or overnight.

Pro Tips

  • All ingredients should be at room temperature for smooth mixing
  • Don't overbeat the mixture to prevent cracks
  • Use a water bath to ensure even, gentle cooking
  • Cool gradually to prevent cracking on the surface
  • Chill overnight for best texture and easier slicing
