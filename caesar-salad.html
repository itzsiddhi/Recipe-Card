Classic Caesar Salad

15 mins 4 servings Easy

Crispy romaine lettuce with homemade dressing and croutons. This classic salad is perfect as a side dish or light meal with its bold, tangy flavors.

Ready to start cooking!
00:00

Ingredients

  • 2 heads romaine lettuce, chopped
  • 1/2 cup parmesan cheese, grated
  • 1 cup croutons
  • 3 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp fresh lemon juice
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp worcestershire sauce
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper
  • 1/4 tsp salt

Instructions

  1. 1
    Prepare lettuce: Wash and thoroughly dry the romaine lettuce, then chop into bite-sized pieces and place in a large bowl.
  2. 2
    Make dressing: In a small bowl, whisk together mayonnaise, lemon juice, minced garlic, worcestershire sauce, salt, and black pepper.
  3. 3
    Dress the lettuce: Pour the dressing over the lettuce and toss well to ensure all leaves are coated.
  4. 4
    Add toppings: Sprinkle the grated parmesan cheese over the dressed lettuce.
  5. 5
    Add croutons: Top with croutons and give everything a final gentle toss.
  6. 6
    Serve immediately: Serve the salad right away for the best texture. Optional: Add grilled chicken for a complete meal.

Pro Tips

  • Dry the lettuce thoroughly for the best texture
  • Make your own croutons for superior flavor
  • Grate parmesan fresh for the best taste
  • Chill the serving bowls beforehand for extra freshness
